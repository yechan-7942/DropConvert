# Quiet Exchange

The icon treats conversion as an exchange of form: two precise paths meet, turn,
and leave as one continuous gesture. The composition is built from a calm,
deep field and a small luminous system, so the idea reads through geometry
before it reads through any label.

Space is generous and deliberate. A rounded square establishes the dependable
container of a native Mac utility; the interlocking arrows create a measured
rhythm inside it without filling the field. Their crossing is the visual
center, while the small central aperture provides a moment of silence and
balance.

Color is material rather than decorative: midnight blue gives the icon weight,
electric cyan suggests input, and warm amber suggests the transformed result.
The restrained palette is tuned for clarity at both menu-bar and Dock scale,
with soft depth used only to keep the mark legible against macOS surfaces.

Every edge, radius, gap, and highlight is meticulously crafted. The final mark
should feel like the product of countless hours of master-level refinement:
simple at first glance, but exact in proportion, spacing, and optical balance.
Text is absent because the form itself communicates the action.
